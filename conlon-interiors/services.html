The process

Four steps, one team.

Measure

Send photos and rough sizes on WhatsApp for a guide price, then we visit to measure properly. Free within Kildare and surrounding counties.

Design

A drawing of the piece in your room, with fire, lighting, colour and panelling choices. One fixed price, nothing added later.

Build

Built in our workshop so the disruption at home is days, not weeks. Spray-painted in any colour you like.

Fit & finish

Our carpenters fit it, connect the fire and lighting, and leave the room clean. Pay by card, transfer or humm.

Finance your project

Spread the cost of bespoke work with humm.

A media wall or fitted wardrobes can be financed through humm up to €30,000, with interest-free plans available. Get pre-approved before your consultation and we’ll build the figures into your quote.

Questions

Bespoke work, answered.

How much does a media wall cost?

Most media walls we build fall between €3,500 and €7,500 depending on size, the electric fire chosen, panelling and lighting. We give a fixed price after measuring.

Do you supply the electric fire?

Yes. We supply and fit fires from leading brands in one-, two- and three-sided styles, or build around a fire you already own.

How long does fitting take?

Because we build in the workshop, most media walls and alcove units are fitted in one to two days. Wardrobes and kitchens take two to five days depending on size.

Where do you cover?

Kildare, Dublin, Meath, Wicklow, Laois, Offaly and Carlow as standard. Further afield by arrangement.

Can I pay with humm for bespoke work?

Yes. Get pre-approved with humm for your quoted amount, and we complete the humm purchase with you when you sign off the design. Interest-free plans are available; terms and eligibility are confirmed by humm.
