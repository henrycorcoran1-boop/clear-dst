Good to know

Delivery, collection and returns.

Plain terms for getting your order home, and what happens if something isn’t right.

Delivery charges

  • €49 to Kildare, Dublin, Meath, Wicklow, Laois, Offaly and Carlow.
  • €89 to the rest of Ireland.
  • Free on orders over €1,500, and always free when you collect from the showroom.

Two-person delivery to the room of your choice. We unwrap, position and take the packaging away on request. Please make sure there is clear access; we will call ahead to agree a day and time.

Lead times

Each product shows its typical lead time. Stocked flooring and doors are usually ready within a week; sofas and beds take two to five weeks; bespoke carpentry is scheduled after your survey and quote. We confirm an expected date when we confirm your order.

Collection

Collect free from Unit 26C Newbridge Industrial Estate, seven days a week. We’ll message you when your order is ready, and we can help load the car or van.

Returns and cancellations

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 and the EU Consumer Rights Directive, you may cancel an online order for stocked goods within 14 days of delivery for any reason. Goods must be unused and in their original packaging; return delivery is at your cost unless the item is faulty, and we refund within 14 days of receiving the goods. Flooring that has been cut or fitted cannot be returned unless faulty.

Made-to-measure and bespoke items (media walls, fitted wardrobes, alcove units, panelling, kitchens, cut-to-size flooring and doors) are made specifically for you and are not covered by the 14-day cancellation right once production has started.

Faulty goods

If anything arrives damaged or develops a fault, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will repair, replace or refund as required under Irish consumer law. Frame guarantees and manufacturer warranties are listed on each product.
