Privacy policy.

We collect the minimum information needed to take your order, deliver it and look after you afterwards.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone, email, delivery address.
  • Order details: what you bought, how you paid, delivery preferences.
  • If you choose humm, your humm acceptance code so we can complete the sale. Your finance application itself is made directly with humm under humm’s privacy policy; we never see your financial details.
  • Basket contents are stored in your own browser (local storage) so they survive a page refresh. They are not sent to us until you place an order.

How we use it

To process and deliver your order, respond to enquiries, arrange surveys and fitting, send order updates, and meet our legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell your data and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Who we share it with

Delivery partners (name, address and phone for delivery only), humm where you choose to pay with humm, our accountants, and any form or email service we use to receive orders. All are bound by GDPR.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six years for tax purposes. Enquiry emails are deleted within two years unless they become orders.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded Google Maps and humm content may set their own cookies when loaded; see their respective policies.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to its use. Email hello@conloninteriors.ie. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland.
