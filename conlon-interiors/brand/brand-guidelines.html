Brand guidelines · v1.0 · October 2026

Built by hand. Presented with care.

How the Conlon Interiors identity works across the website, social media, print and the showroom. Keep to these and everything we put out looks like it came from the same workshop.

01

The idea

Conlon Interiors is a carpentry business first. The identity keeps the honesty of the original signage (bold condensed capitals, the orange rule, the hammer) and dresses it in premium interiors cues: charcoal, oak, linen, a serif for headlines and plenty of space. Confident, warm, never flashy.

DOClear space & minimum size

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the capital “C”.
  • Minimum width: 120 px on screen, 30 mm in print for the wordmark; 24 px / 8 mm for the monogram.
  • Use the white version on photos and dark backgrounds, charcoal on light.

DON’TAlter the mark

  • Don’t stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Don’t recolour the bars anything other than ember orange, oak or the mono colour.
  • Don’t place the charcoal logo on busy photos without a solid panel behind it.
  • Don’t rebuild the service list columns with other words.

05

Photography

Finished rooms, straight on
Shoot the piece square-on from the middle of the room, lights on, fire on, TV off or on a calm screen.
Material close-ups
Flooring, panelling and door details in natural daylight. Let the grain do the talking.
Inside the build
Open wardrobe interiors, workshop shots, hands on tools. Shows the carpentry, not just the finish.

Avoid heavy filters, clutter and phone screenshots. Tidy the room, square the camera, and crop to 1:1 for the grid and 4:5 for feature posts.

06

Tone of voice

Plain, confident, a bit of Kildare warmth. We talk like a tradesperson who knows the job, not like a furniture chain. Short sentences. Real numbers. No “luxury lifestyle” fluff.

Say this“Media wall measured Monday, drawn Tuesday, fitted three weeks later. One fixed price, nothing added.”
Not this“Elevate your living space with our luxurious bespoke entertainment solutions, tailored to your unique lifestyle.”
Say this“Spread the cost with humm. €1,499 sofa, from €124.92 a month over 12 months, 0% interest.”
Not this“Flexible finance solutions available, T&Cs apply.”
AlwaysNever
Name the town (Newbridge, Kildare)“Nationwide leaders in…”
Give a from-price or a monthly figure“Prices on request” with nothing else
Show the humm warning text with any finance claimFinance claims without the regulatory line
Sign off with a phone number and the showroom hours“DM for info”
