How it works

Three steps to spreading the cost.

Apply with humm

Head to apply.humm.ie and apply for the amount you’d like to spend. You’ll need photo ID, your PPS number, proof of address and a bank card in your name. Most decisions take about five minutes.

Choose humm at checkout

Online, pick “Spread the cost with humm” at checkout and enter your acceptance code. In the showroom, just tell us you’d like to pay with humm and have your phone ready.

Pay in slices

Your purchase must be equal to or less than your approved amount. Repayments are taken automatically from your card, fortnightly or monthly, over the term you choose.

Who can apply

  • 18 years or over
  • Permanent resident or citizen of Ireland
  • Minimum income of €1,500 a month
  • Good credit history
  • Photo ID, PPS number, proof of address, bank card in your name

Eligibility is set and assessed by humm. Check your eligibility.

Fees and rates

Interest rates run from 0% to 14.99% per annum depending on the plan. Application fees range from €10 to €50 and account-keeping fees from €1 to €20 per month, depending on the plan. A €9 dishonour fee applies if a payment is missed and not resolved. You can repay early at no extra charge.

Representative example (humm): €800 over 12 months at 4.99% variable, €15 application fee, €2/month account-keeping fee: APR 14.90%, total cost of credit €60.77, total payable €860.77.

Figures published by humm Ireland and confirmed at application. Interest-free plans are available on selected terms.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I use humm for bespoke work like a media wall?

Yes. Get pre-approved for the quoted amount and we’ll complete the humm purchase with you when you confirm the design.

Is there a deposit?

Depending on the plan, your first instalment may be taken when the purchase is completed. humm shows the schedule before you confirm.

Can I buy part of my order with humm?

In the showroom, yes: you can part-pay with humm and the rest by card. Online orders use humm for the full amount.

What if I already have a humm account?

Log in at myaccount.humm.ie to check your available limit, then shop as normal.

Who provides the credit?

humm is a trading name of Flexifi Europe Limited, regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland. Conlon Interiors introduces customers to humm and does not provide credit itself.
