Our story

“I’m a qualified carpenter, not a content creator.”

That line from our Facebook page says most of it. We’d rather be in the workshop than in front of a camera, and it shows in the work.

Newbridge, Co. Kildare

A carpentry workshop with a showroom attached.

Conlon Interiors started as a carpentry business: feature walls, fitted units, wardrobes and panelling, built properly and fitted by the people who made them. The showroom in Newbridge Industrial Estate grew out of customers asking us where to get the flooring, doors and furniture to finish the room.

Today you can walk in seven days a week, run your hand over the flooring samples, sit on the sofas, pick a door, and talk to the carpenter who’ll build your media wall. Everything is priced plainly and you can spread the cost with humm.

What we stand for

One fixed price.
