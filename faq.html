Clarifications

Frequently Asked

Anonymity, ethics approval, and how the report is generated.

Is my response anonymous?
Yes. No individual or organisation is identifiable from the data. Your answers are processed entirely in your own browser to generate your report — they are not uploaded, stored on a server, or linked to your identity in any way.
Has this study received ethical approval?
Yes. This study has received ethical approval from the UCD Human Research Ethics Committee, as part of doctoral research into innovation culture in construction project organisations.
How are the dimension scores calculated?
Each of the 36 Section 1 items is rated 1–5. Items are grouped into four dimensions (Leadership, Environment, Architecture, Resources — 9 items each) and averaged. Scores below 3.0 are banded RED ("Needs Attention"), 3.0–3.69 AMBER ("Developing"), and 3.7+ GREEN ("Strength").
How are the recommended interventions chosen?
Your five lowest-scoring items — regardless of dimension — are surfaced first, each paired with a specific, practical intervention for that indicator. Dimension-level summaries then add strategic framing based on the overall band for Leadership, Environment, Architecture and Resources.
What does Section 2 add?
Section 2 asks about your professional background and, where relevant, your experience of leaving or seriously considering leaving a construction role. If you answer these questions, your report will link your departure reasons and knowledge-transfer experience directly back to the LEAR dimension they map to — showing culture's connection to real turnover cost, not just an abstract score.
What if I've never considered leaving a construction role?
Select that option at question B1 and the survey will automatically skip the departure-specific questions (B2–B4, C1, C4) — you'll only be asked the two questions that apply to everyone regardless of departure history.
Is my data saved if I close the browser partway through?
Your in-progress answers are saved to your browser's local storage so you can resume later on the same device and browser. Nothing leaves your device until you choose to print or export your finished report.
Can I use CLEAR-DST for my own organisation or team?
The instrument is designed to be completed by individuals reflecting on a current or recent project. For an organisation-wide diagnostic, have multiple team members complete it independently and compare dimension scores and priority interventions across responses.
