The LEAR Framework

Four Conditions. One Diagnostic.

Each dimension is anchored in an established organisational theory, then translated into observable, project-level statements your team rates from 1–5.

Leadership

Edmondson (1999) · Psychological safety & idea cultivation

Environment

Nahapiet & Ghoshal (1998) · Social capital between stakeholders

Architecture

O'Reilly & Tushman (2013) · Structural ambidexterity

Resources

Nohria & Gulati (1996) · Organisational slack
From Score to Action

A Bespoke Report — Not Just a Score

Completing the survey generates a report specific to your answers: dimension-by-dimension scoring against RED / AMBER / GREEN thresholds, the five priority interventions ranked by weakest indicator, and — where departure or turnover data is provided — a direct link between culture and workforce cost.

Why It Matters

Culture Has a Cost Line

Section 2 of the survey links departure and retention experience to the LEAR dimensions — so a low Architecture score isn't just a diagnostic footnote, it's connected to knowledge loss, vacancy duration and disengagement cost.

Built for construction project organisations

  • 36-item innovation readiness instrument across four LEAR dimensions
  • 13-item background & departure module for turnover-cost attribution
  • Instant, bespoke intervention report — no waiting on a consultant
  • Fully anonymous, ethically approved (UCD Human Research Ethics Committee)

From Diagnosis to Action

Every dimension score is paired with the specific, practical intervention linked to your lowest-scoring indicators — not generic advice. Interventions are grounded in the same organisational literature that anchors the framework.
